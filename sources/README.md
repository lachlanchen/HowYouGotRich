# Source Records

`interviews.csv` indexes the 135 School of Hard Knocks videos used by the book.
Each row provides the public video identity and URL, final transcript timestamp,
word count, and SHA-256 checksum of the transcript used for the accepted
edition. The transcript text is not redistributed here.

`figures.csv` records every documentary frame published in the book, including
its source video, timestamp, interviewee, caption, and verification status.
The image files themselves live beside the TeX under
`source/book/manuscript/figures/`.

These records support corrections and attribution. They do not imply that
School of Hard Knocks or any interviewee reviewed or endorsed the book.
