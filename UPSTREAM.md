# Publication Provenance

This repository is the curated publication surface for *How You Got Rich*.
The broader source project is
[`lachlanchen/LazyEarn`](https://github.com/lachlanchen/LazyEarn), with book
development on its `wealth-book-polish` branch.

The first standalone snapshot was exported from LazyEarn commit
`f2ea57d09e1f17b189c685923c86938a2d98559c`. The manuscript and accepted PDF
content were established at commit
`49e051a3cd807edaa19d8dd0017e0fa785d08b61`.

A publication update should copy only:

- canonical TeX and direct book assets;
- accepted documentary figures and public attribution metadata;
- validated full-size and pocket PDFs;
- the first-page preview rendered from the accepted full-size PDF; and
- reader-facing documentation and reproducible build tools.

Raw media, transcripts, private records, caches, credentials, downloaded
research books, and temporary build trees do not belong in this repository.
